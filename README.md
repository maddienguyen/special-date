# ⚔️ ULTRAKILL Date Proposal // Sahil & Maddie 💖

A retro-cyberpunk, fast-paced date proposal website styled after the hit indie FPS **ULTRAKILL** (with an exclusive **Iron Man 2** movie night finale!).

---

## 🚀 The Date Itinerary

1. **Stage 01 — ULTRAKILL Level Beating & Friendly Duel**:
   * Sahil coaches Maddie on sliding, projectile boosting, coin ricoshots, and parrying Gabriel.
   * Includes a playable in-browser **Marksman Coin Shooting Simulator** (Spacebar / click) with hit streaks and style points!
   * Campaign level clearance checklist and live Sahil vs Maddie Cybergrind duel scoreboard.

2. **Stage 02 — Vision Board on Canva**:
   * Direct launch button to Canva's collaborative Vision Board Maker.
   * Features all 9 guiding questions and 8 future blueprint categories (Dream Home, Travel, Cars, Battlestations, Pets, Kids & Baby Names).
   * Interactive scratchpad pinboard to type and pin romantic quotes and future dreams.

3. **Stage 03 — Anime Wheel of Judgement**:
   * Interactive physics-based canvas spinning wheel with authentic in-game peg-clicking sound effects.
   * Chooses between **Black Clover**, **Cyberpunk: Edgerunners**, or **Dragon Ball**.
   * Automatic episode generator recommending high-octane standout episodes.

4. **Stage 04 — Iron Man 2 Movie Night (Exclusive Stark Industries Theme)**:
   * Only this page transforms into Tony Stark's Hot-Rod Crimson (`#d1121f`), Stark Gold (`#ffd700`), and glowing Arc Reactor Cyan (`#00f0ff`) with JARVIS HUD panels.
   * Interactive pulsing Arc Reactor core (click to overcharge).
   * Pre-flight movie checklist, Monaco Grand Prix suitcase armor review, and AC/DC vibes.
