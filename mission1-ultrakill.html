V1 COMBAT DOCTRINE

[LESSON 01]

BLOOD IS FUEL (HEALING)

There are no medkits in ULTRAKILL. To regain HP, get right in the enemy's face and shower in fresh blood. Sliding keeps your hitbox tiny while maintaining high momentum!

[LESSON 02]

MARKSMAN COIN MASTERY

Toss a coin into the air with right-click, then shoot it with your revolver or pierce it with the railcannon. The bullet auto-targets enemy weakpoints for insane multiplier damage!

[LESSON 03]

PROJECTILE BOOSTING

Fire the primary shotgun blast and hit 'F' (Feedbacker punch) in the exact same millisecond. You punch your own pellets, turning them into a massive flying explosive mortar!

[LESSON 04]

PARRYING GABRIEL & V2

When an attack flashes yellow, punch it directly to parry! Parrying fully restores your stamina and health to 100% instantly while chunking the boss's lifebar.
